# Sock animation — every sock is alive

Every sock moves on its own, in a way that depends on its **type** (21 personalities), its
**rarity**, its **mutation**, its **situation** and **what is happening around it**. All of it is
procedural (no animation assets) and client-side: `Bone.Transform` and the client-moved root part
are never replicated, so it costs the server nothing.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `src/client/StealASockClient/SockAnim.luau` | the engine: one `RenderStepped` loop for every sock, the moves, reactions, sounds |
| `src/shared/Config/SockAnimConfig.luau` | every tuning knob: personalities, rarity energy, mutation flavour, joint limits, reactions, sound ids, performance |
| `src/shared/Config/SockRigConfig.luau` | GENERATED by the Blender kit (`tools/blender/rigging.py`): the bones in every sock mesh, their roles and clean ranges |
| `src/client/StealASockClient/SockFX.luau` | still owns the clothesline maths, bursts, outlines, the dryer; hands the sock motion to SockAnim (`SetDriver`) and keeps it for any sock SockAnim can't animate |

## Layers

Every frame, for every sock, these are added together:

1. **The situation**
   - *In a drawer* (and in the menus): the personality's **idle loop**.
   - *On the clothesline*: its **hang style** — pinned at the clothespin, it kicks, wiggles and
     swings; within 16 studs of a player (someone who could buy it) it **perks up**: kicks harder and
     swings toward them. Every so often a big hanging wiggle. At the end of the line it flails into
     the Lint Trap with a squeak.
   - *Carried on a thief's head*: **struggling** — flailing, kicking, bending back toward its home
     drawer, squeaking now and then.
2. **Fidgets and show-offs**: a little one-shot every few seconds (from the personality's list), and
   for Rare and up a **show-off** now and then — its signature move, or (Epic+) a rarity flair move:
   spin, flip, pose — with a sparkle in the rarity colour. Secret socks glitch, stare and flicker.
3. **Mutation flavour** (always on, on top).
4. **Reactions** (world socks only, see below).
5. **Moments**: big one-shots that interrupt everything else (see below).

Then: joints are clamped to the rig's clean range, googly pupils roll on their springs, and it is all
written to the bones and the root part.

## How the bones are driven

- **Bones are found by name** anywhere under the sock model (the importer may nest the meshes a
  level deeper, and the body and its `_Outline` hull may each carry their own copy of the
  skeleton). Every Bone with the same name gets the same motion.
- Roles come from `SockRigConfig` (`Types[typeId][boneName].role`); without it, from the names
  (`Root`, `Leg1..4`, `Cuff`, `Foot`, `Toe`, `Toe1..5`, `Eye1/2`, `Tent<i>_<j>`, `Neck1..4`, `Head`,
  `Jaw`, `Hat`, `Pogo`, `Fly1..3`, `Hump1/2`, `Tail`, `Lake`, anything else = accessory).
- Every move is written as rotations in the **sock's own axes** — up (toward the cuff), face (+Z,
  where the eyes look) and toe (+X on a right sock, -X on a left one, so **L and R are mirrored
  automatically**) — about each bone's rest pivot. Each Bone's `Transform` is
  `rest⁻¹ · motion · rest`, using its rest frame in sock space, so **bone roll never matters**.
- A bend of the leg is spread along `Leg1..Leg4 + Cuff` by a profile (base-heavy … top-heavy) and by
  segment length (Ankle Biter's tiny Leg2 / Leg3 bend very little).
- **Standing** (drawer, menu, on a head): the **foot is planted** — the Foot bone keeps its own
  rotation whatever Leg1 does, and forward kinematics finds where the foot would have gone; the
  Root bone shifts everything back by that much.
- **Hanging**: the same forward kinematics follows the **clothespin point** (the `_Pin` marker,
  riding the Cuff); the Root bone shifts it back, so the cuff stays on the line and the foot swings.
  `hangSwing` bends the leg so the cuff keeps its angle while the foot end swings.
- **Whole-body motion** (hops, spins, flips, sways) moves the root part (`Leg`) in the world, so the
  clothespin, labels, rarity halos and mutation parts come along. In the menus the `Root` bone
  carries it instead (the menus may turn the model themselves).
- **Joint limits** (`SockAnimConfig.Limits`, from the rig's own tests): 15° per leg joint (10° for
  Socktopus / The Lost Sock, 8.6° for Sockrates and Sir Sockington, whose beard / armour are long and
  rigid), 10° of twist per joint (more turns the whole sock), tight hats < 8°, the jaws inside the
  rig's `slide` / `turn` (talking is mostly a slide).
- **Special bones**: `Eye1/2` carry only the pupils — rolling them makes **googly eyes** that stay
  "down" as the sock tips, rattle when it hops and **swing toward the player it looks at**;
  Toe-Toe's five toes never stop wiggling; Stinkolino's flies buzz round; the Sock Ness humps and
  tail sway in its suds puddle, and the puddle (`Lake`, role `ground`) **stays on the floor** whatever
  the sock does (it undoes the Root shift and the body motion).
- **No bones** (Part-built socks, an old place) or **far away**: the same layers fold into
  whole-body motion of the root part, so a far sock still bobs, sways and spins like itself.

## Reactions and their triggers

| Reaction | Trigger | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look | the nearest player within 30 studs | the upper body turns toward them, the face tips to meet their eyes, the googly pupils swing toward them; a little "oh, hi!" hop when someone new comes into view |
| Perk up | clothesline sock, a player within 16 studs | kicks faster and swings toward them |
| Panic | another player is inside **your** drawer (only your socks) | shiver, lean away from the thief, flail now and then (squeak) |
| Pair bop | `Paired` with its other half in the same drawer | turn toward each other and bop together on a shared 96 BPM beat |
| Flinch | a Towel Snap (FX `"snap"`) within 25 studs | jerk away from it; the nearest one says "oof" |
| Cheer | a Legendary+ sock bought or stolen anywhere (FX `"buy"` / `"claim"` in that tier's colour) | every sock near you jumps and waves, a ripple not a chorus line; the two nearest make the noise |
| Sleep | drawer sock, nobody within 40 studs for 45 s | droops, slow breathing, floating "Zzz", a soft snore (the nearest one) |
| Wake | someone comes back near a dozing sock | a startled hop and squeak |

## Performance and settings

- One `RenderStepped` loop for every sock (and the menu socks). No per-frame tables or closures;
  bones whose motion is zero are skipped; a bone shared by the body and its hull is solved once.
- Bones only within **120 studs** of the camera (whole-body motion beyond), **every other frame
  beyond 60**, whole-body motion every 4th frame beyond 250. At most **40** socks bend at once:
  past that the bone range shrinks until it fits (with a little hysteresis, so nothing flickers).
- Reactions are scanned ~7 times a second per sock, the player list 10 times a second.
- **Low graphics**: bones only within 50 studs, half the sparkles. **Reduce motion**: everything at
  half size, no spins or flips, glitch jumps tiny. **Hide others' effects**: no "Zzz" on other
  players' socks, and no show-off sparkles on theirs farther than 30 studs from the camera.
- Menu socks: only while their viewport is on screen; every other frame when more than 8 show.
- A sock model leaving the game (sold, despawned, streamed out) is cleaned up: bones reset,
  sounds / sparkles / Zzz removed, connections dropped.
- If a sock ever errors, SockAnim stops animating just that one (a warning in Output) and SockFX
  moves it the old way.

## API (for the menus)

```lua
SockAnim.Init()
local handle = SockAnim.Attach(model, "ui", { typeId = "Argylo", side = "L", rarity = "Rare", mutation = "None" })
handle:Destroy() -- stops it and puts the pose back
SockAnim.Moment(model, "showoff") -- or "bought", "stolen", "home", "paired", "goodbye", "cheer", "flinch", any fidget
SockAnim.Goodbye(uid) -- right before selling a drawer sock
```

Attach expects the menu copy of a `ReplicatedStorage.SockMeshes` template (front +Z, pivot on the
floor point, as `SockCard` prepares it), inside a `WorldModel` (bones only deform there). Its idle
and the odd show-off play; no reactions, no sounds.

## Server hooks

- `Pair.Recompute` sets the model attributes **`Paired`** (bool) and **`PairUid`** (the other half's
  uid, 0 = none) on every sock in the drawer.
- `Steal.TryPickup` clears them on the stolen sock (so a reunion dances again).
- `Factory` removes any `Animator` / `AnimationController` an import brought along (it would overwrite
  `Bone.Transform` every frame); the Bones themselves are kept as they are.
- Nothing else: buys, steals, returns and claims are read from the `State` attribute; cheers and
  flinches from the existing FX remote.

## What to check in Studio

1. **Bones imported?** Select a sock in `ReplicatedStorage.SockMeshes` (e.g. `Argylo_R`): under its
   MeshParts there should be `Bone`s named `Root`, `Leg1`…`Leg4`, `Cuff`, `Foot`, `Toe`, `Eye1`,
   `Eye2`. If not, re-import the GLB with the rig (File → Import 3D; keep "Merge Meshes" off).
2. **Play and watch a drawer sock up close**: the leg should curve (not just tip as a block), the foot
   should stay on its cushion, the googly pupils should roll. A sock that only tips as a whole
   has no bones (or is beyond the bone range).
3. **Does the black outline bend with the body?** The `_Outline` hull is skinned to the same bones.
   If it tears, lags or pokes through at the joints, set `SockAnimConfig.OutlineMode = "highlight"`:
   the hulls are hidden and mesh socks get the same Highlight outline as Part socks (nearest 22, or
   8 with Low graphics; menu viewports can't draw Highlights, so menu socks then have none).
4. **Clothesline**: a hanging sock's clothespin should stay on the rope while its foot swings.
5. **Creases**: if a joint creases, lower `Limits.Bend` (or that type's `bend`) a little.
6. **Output**: `[SockAnim]` warnings name anything it couldn't animate (it hands those socks back to
   SockFX, so the game keeps working).
7. **Performance**: with a full server, check the MicroProfiler (`Ctrl+F6`) for `RenderStepped`. If
   it is heavy on phones, lower `Perf.MaxBoneSocks` or `Perf.BoneRange`.

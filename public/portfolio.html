03 — About

The person behind the lens.

I'm Josh — a one-person crew with a director-of-photography eye. I shoot video, photographs and cinema-style projects, and I care as much about the edit and the color grade as I do about the day of the shoot.

Whatever the format, the job is the same: find the light, wait for the honest moment, and frame it like it matters. I keep productions light on their feet, so the people in front of the camera stay relaxed and the work stays true.

i.

Pre-production

Creative direction, shot lists, locations and scheduling — so shoot day is calm, not chaotic.

ii.

Production

Cinema cameras, deliberate lighting and a documentary instinct for the unscripted moment.

iii.

Post

Editing, sound and color grading, delivered in every format and crop the work needs to live in.

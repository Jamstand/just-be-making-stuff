( about me )

the person behind the camera.

I'm Josh. I shoot video, photos, and film projects, usually as a one-person crew, and honestly I like it that way. Small footprint, relaxed shoots, nobody standing around holding a clipboard.

My whole approach fits in one sentence: find the light, wait for the real moment, and frame it like it matters. Then I go home and fuss over the edit way longer than I plan to.

1.

We talk

You tell me the idea. I ask a lot of questions and start scribbling shot lists on whatever's nearby.

2.

We shoot

Real cameras, deliberate light, and enough patience to catch the stuff nobody planned.

3.

I disappear into the edit

Cutting, sound, and color until it feels like the day actually felt. You get every format you need.

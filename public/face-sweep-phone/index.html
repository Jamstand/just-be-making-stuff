Delete, hide or sort a person's photos: the fast way

Your phone's Photos app already groups faces, sees your whole library, and can delete, hide or make an album in a few taps, with a confirmation at each step. Use it for the big job. Use Face Sweep below to double-check a batch, or to find someone your Photos app hasn't grouped.

iPhone: Photos app
  1. Open Photos and find People & Pets: on iOS 26 or later tap Collections at the bottom; on iOS 18 swipe up below your photos; on iOS 17 tap Albums.
  2. Tap the person. If they aren't named yet, tap Name This Person (iOS 17: Add Name). On iOS 17 also tap Show More so every photo is listed.
  3. Tap Select (top right). If Select All appears at the top left, tap it. Otherwise slide your finger across the photos to select them in runs.
  4. Then choose one:
    • Delete: tap the trash icon and confirm. They wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone.
    • Hide: tap …, then Hide. They move to the Hidden collection (under Utilities), locked with Face ID.
    • Separate album: tap … (on iOS 26, the Share icon), then Add to Album, then create a new album.

Deleting from a person's page deletes from your whole library (and iCloud). If a photo is wrongly tagged, open or select it and tap … → This is Not [name] instead. If the same person shows up as two faces, open People & Pets, tap Edit (older: Select), pick both, then Merge.

Android: Google Photos
  1. Open Google Photos, tap Collections (bottom), then People & pets, and tap the face. Newer versions also show faces at the top of Search.
  2. Touch and hold the first photo, then drag your finger down the grid to select a run, or tap the others. There is no Select all.
  3. Then choose one:
    • Delete: tap Trash (bottom) and confirm. Trash keeps them for 30 days.
    • Hide: tap ⋮ (top right), then Archive: gone from the main grid, still in albums and search. For real hiding tap Add to (bottom), then Move to Locked Folder: gone from everywhere, behind your screen lock (set it up once under Collections → Locked). To delete or share them later, move them out of the Locked Folder first.
    • Separate album: tap Add to album (under Add to in newer versions) and give it a title. Even easier: open any album, tap ⋮ → Options → Add, pick the person's face, and Google Photos keeps that album filled with their photos by itself.

No faces showing? Tap your profile picture → Photos settings → Privacy → turn on Face Groups, then give it time to group. Google doesn't offer it in every region.

Samsung Gallery
  1. Open Gallery, tap Collections (older phones: Albums), scroll to People and pets and tap the person. Missing? Turn on Face recognition in Gallery's settings (Menu → Settings) and give it time to scan.
  2. Touch and hold a photo, then tap the others (or All at the top, where offered).
  3. Tap Delete → Move to Trash (kept 30 days), or tap ⋮ for Move to Secure Folder (hide) or Create album.

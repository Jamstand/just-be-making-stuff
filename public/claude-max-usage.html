05 — Tips

Stretching the budget, gently.

Three small habits that keep both clocks honest, without changing the work you do.

i.

Right-size the model.

Drafting, search, formatting? Sonnet or Haiku. Reach for Opus when the problem is actually hard — long-context reasoning, gnarly bugs, the writing that takes care.

ii.

Reuse the project, not the prompt.

Projects keep instructions and files in one place, so cached context does the work that re-prompting otherwise repeats. Cheaper per turn, and the answers stay in tune.

iii.

Mind the five-hour window.

Long parallel sessions are what trip the short window first. Stagger heavy runs by 30–60 minutes and the weekly budget tends to follow the line on its own.

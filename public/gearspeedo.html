What you're looking at

The widget is drawn here by the same layout logic as the in-game Lua app: an 18-segment rev bar that runs green, amber, then red, and flashes white once revs pass the shift point. The gear number goes red at the same moment. Everything scales with the window, so dragging the corner is exactly what resizing does in the game.

The car is a simulated MX-5 ND: full throttle up through the gears, a bounce off the limiter in fifth, a hard braking zone with downshifts, then a reverse out of the pit box. The replay loops.

Install

Copy apps/lua/GearSpeedo into <AC root>/apps/lua/. It appears under Your apps in the Custom Shaders Patch app list; nothing to switch on. Needs CSP build 2512 or newer.

Gear convention

CSP reports the gear directly: negative is reverse, zero is neutral, one and up is the gear itself. The Python build of this app uses AC's older API, which offsets by one.

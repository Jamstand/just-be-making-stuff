How it works v.

Two layers of AI: MediaPipe runs in your browser and masks you from the background every frame, so background blur / replace are real and live. Claude watches a snapshot and picks the colour grading parameters that the canvas applies on top.

Edit photos live

This app connects to your camera and re-grades every frame in real time, using Claude to interpret your prompt as filter parameters.

Drop photos here

Each photo gets sent to Claude, which picks colour-grading filters — brightness, contrast, hue, tint, grain, glow, vignette. The canvas applies them and you get a preview.

Filter grading only. It can't remove objects (reflections, people, signage), repaint pixels, or change the colour of just one thing in the frame — that needs a generative tool like Photoshop Generative Fill.

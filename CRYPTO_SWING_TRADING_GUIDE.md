# Crypto Swing Trading Guide

A beginner's guide to swing trading cryptocurrency around a full-time work schedule.

---

## Who This Is For

This guide is written for someone who:

- Works Monday–Friday, roughly 7am–5pm (home by 5–6pm EST at the earliest)
- Cannot watch markets during US stock market hours (9:30am–4pm EST)
- Wants to start trading but has limited time and capital
- Is a complete beginner with no prior trading experience

Standard stock market trading is a poor fit for this schedule — the market closes at 4pm EST, before you're even home. **Crypto swing trading** solves this problem.

---

## Why Crypto Swing Trading

- **24/7 markets** — crypto never closes, so your evenings and weekends are fully usable
- **No Pattern Day Trader (PDT) rule** — stocks require a $25,000 minimum account balance to day trade; crypto has no such rule
- **Swing trading = positions held days to weeks** — no need to watch a screen all day
- **Schedule fit** — weekend analysis and weekday passive monitoring aligns naturally with a 9-to-5 life

## Getting Started

### Exchanges

- **Coinbase** — most beginner-friendly, easiest to set up
- **Kraken** — slightly better trading tools, good for intermediate use

### Charting

- **TradingView** — free charting platform with paper trading (simulated trades with no real money)

### Starting Capital

- Start with **$500–$1,000** of real money
- **Paper trade on TradingView for at least 4 weeks** before using real money
- Never trade money needed for bills or living expenses

### What to Trade

- Start with **Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH) only**
- Avoid altcoins, leverage, options, and margin until you are consistently profitable

## The Core Strategy

A basic swing trade setup follows these steps:

1. Find a coin in an **uptrend** — characterized by higher highs and higher lows on the chart
2. Wait for a **pullback** to a support level or the 50-day moving average
3. Watch for **RSI to drop to 40–50** during the pullback (indicating temporary weakness, not reversal)
4. **Enter** when price shows signs of bouncing (e.g., a strong green candle, volume spike)
5. Set a **stop loss** just below the support level before entering the trade
6. Set a **take profit** at the next identifiable resistance level

## Key Indicators to Learn

| Indicator | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Candlestick charts** | Green = price went up, Red = price went down for that period |
| **Support & Resistance** | Price levels where buying/selling pressure historically clusters (floor/ceiling) |
| **50-day Moving Average (MA)** | Short-to-medium term trend direction |
| **200-day Moving Average (MA)** | Long-term trend direction |
| **RSI (Relative Strength Index)** | Momentum; above 70 = overbought, below 30 = oversold |
| **Volume** | Confirms the strength of price moves — high volume = conviction |

## Risk Management Rules

These rules are non-negotiable. Breaking them is how accounts blow up.

- Risk only **1–2% of your account per trade** — on a $500 account, that's $5–$10 per trade
- **Always set a stop loss before entering** — never enter without knowing your exit
- Aim for a **2:1 reward-to-risk ratio minimum** — if you risk $10, your target profit should be at least $20
- **Never move a stop loss down** to avoid taking a loss
- **Never add to a losing position**
- **Stop trading for the day after 3 consecutive losses**

> A 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio means you only need to be right about 40% of the time to be profitable over many trades.

## Weekly Routine

### Friday Night / Saturday

- Review BTC and ETH charts on TradingView
- Identify potential setups — plan entry price, stop loss, and take profit **in advance**
- Do not force a trade if no clear setup exists

### Sunday

- Place orders if the setup is still valid and confirmed
- Set stop losses and take profit targets at the same time as the entry

### Weekday Evenings (15 minutes)

- Check whether the trade is playing out as expected
- If in profit, consider trailing the stop loss upward to lock in gains
- **Do not second-guess the plan** — trust the analysis done over the weekend

## Realistic Expectations

- **Year 1** — focus on learning the craft; aim to break even; expect some losses
- **Year 2** — small, consistent profits; grow the account steadily
- **Year 3+** — meaningful returns with discipline and compounding

**Industry reality:** 70–80% of retail traders lose money in year 1. Treat year 1 as paying tuition, not making income.

**Realistic monthly target once profitable:** 2–3% per month

### Compounding Example (starting with $1,000 at 2.5%/month)

| Milestone | Account Value |
|---|---|
| Month 6 | ~$1,194 |
| Year 1 | ~$1,426 |
| Year 2 | ~$2,032 |
| Year 3 | ~$2,898 |

**Experienced traders (3+ years):** 15–50%+ annual returns are considered good. Income depends heavily on **account size × consistent win rate**.
